### 1. The Caribbean

The quintessential cruise destination, the Caribbean offers a diverse tapestry of islands, each with its own unique culture and landscape. From the vibrant coral reefs of the Bahamas to the lush rainforests of St. Lucia, a Caribbean cruise is a perfect introduction to the joys of sea travel. Most itineraries are 7 nights, giving you plenty of time to relax and explore.

### 2. Alaska

For those who prefer majestic scenery over sandy beaches, an Alaskan cruise is an unparalleled experience. Witness towering glaciers calve into the sea, spot whales and bald eagles, and explore charming frontier towns. The calm waters of the Inside Passage make for smooth sailing, ideal for first-timers.

### 3. The Mediterranean

Immerse yourself in history, art, and culinary delights with a Mediterranean cruise. A single trip can take you from the ancient ruins of Rome and Athens to the stunning coastlines of the Greek Isles and the French Riviera. It's a fantastic way to see multiple European countries without the hassle of trains and planes.

### 4. The Mexican Riviera

Departing from the West Coast of the United States, a cruise to the Mexican Riviera offers a vibrant and festive atmosphere. Enjoy beautiful beaches, world-class water sports, and delicious local cuisine in ports like Cabo San Lucas, Puerto Vallarta, and Mazatlán. These shorter cruises are a great way to test the waters.

### 5. Norwegian Fjords

Sailing from Southampton, the Norwegian Fjords offer some of the most dramatic and breathtaking landscapes on earth. Glide through deep, tranquil fjords flanked by towering cliffs and cascading waterfalls. It's a serene and visually stunning journey that feels like stepping into a fairytale, and it's easily accessible from the UK.
